Advertisers

Buying calls and leads.

What do I actually pay for?
Calls that pass your buffer and qualification criteria, and leads that pass validation, consent check, and dedup. Everything else is free. Full mechanics on the pricing model page.
How do I know the leads aren't resold?
Exclusive delivery, duplicate detection against your history, and source-chain transparency on every record — audit it yourself in the dashboard. See lead & call quality.
How fast is launch?
5–10 business days from signed IO to first call, mapped day by day on how it works.
Will this pass our compliance review?
That's what the entire Trust & compliance section is for — send it to your legal team, then send us their questions.
Publishers

Sending traffic.

How fast do applications get reviewed?
2–3 business days for complete applications. Apply here — vague traffic descriptions are the main cause of delay.
When do I get paid?
Net-15 standard, weekly available for established partners, $100 minimum threshold. All published on payouts & terms.
What traffic is prohibited?
Incentivized traffic, aged data re-dialed as fresh, undisclosed rebrokering, and anything without evidencable consent. Full list on requirements.
Why was my account paused?
Pauses follow the enforcement table on requirements — your account manager will have the specific trigger and the remediation path in writing.
