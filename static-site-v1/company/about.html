About CCM

Built by people who got burned by bad lead networks.

CCM exists because both sides of this market deserved better: advertisers tired of paying for recycled data, and publishers tired of opaque payouts. We built the network we wished we could have joined.

Mission

Connect real intent to real capacity — compliantly.

Every consumer in our funnel asked to be contacted. Every advertiser gets evidence, not assurances. Every publisher gets terms they can read on a public page. That's the whole model; the rest is execution.

Values

How we decide when it's hard.

  • Publish the terms. Payouts, return policies, requirements — if we'd only say it privately, we shouldn't be doing it.
  • Quality over volume, every collision. We've cut revenue to cut a bad source. It's why the good sources stay.
  • Compliance is a feature. Not a department that says no — the reason enterprise buyers can say yes.
  • Named humans. Account managers, compliance owners, escalation contacts — accountability has a name here.
