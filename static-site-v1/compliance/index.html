Trust & Compliance

The compliance framework.

This section is written for the person doing diligence on us — a compliance officer, in-house counsel, or carrier audit team. It describes how the program is structured, who owns it, and where the detail lives. No sales copy from here down.

Structure

How the program is organized.

Ownership

The compliance program is owned by a named internal compliance lead reporting to executive management, with outside counsel engaged for regulatory review.

Written standards

Publisher obligations, consent requirements, and creative standards are contractual — set out in the publisher agreement and advertiser terms, not informal guidelines.

Preventive controls

Publisher vetting before activation, creative pre-approval in sensitive verticals, filter enforcement before routing, and DNC scrubbing before any outbound contact.

Detective controls

Per-event quality scoring, fraud-signal monitoring, duplicate detection, and recorded-call sampling — automated flags with human investigation.

Corrective process

Documented warning, pause, and removal paths for publishers; credit and clawback mechanics for affected advertisers; regulator-inquiry response procedure.
