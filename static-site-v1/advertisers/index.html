FAQ

Common questions from advertisers.

What counts as a billable call?
A call becomes billable only after it passes your agreed buffer (for example, 90 seconds of connected time) and meets the qualification criteria set at campaign launch. Everything else is free. Full detail on the pricing model page.
Are your leads exclusive?
Yes. Leads generated for your campaign are delivered to you alone, in real time. We run duplicate detection against prior deliveries so you never pay twice for the same consumer. See lead & call quality.
How fast can we launch?
Typical onboarding runs 5–10 business days from signed IO to first delivered call, depending on integration complexity. The how it works page walks through week one.
Can you post into our CRM and dialer?
Yes — real-time posting via API or webhook into common CRMs, dialers, and tracking platforms. See integrations for the current list and posting specs.
