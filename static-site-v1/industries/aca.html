FAQ

ACA questions.

How do you prevent agent-of-record problems?
Consent is captured one-to-one for the buyer who will enroll the consumer, with language and timestamp retained. We don't broker consent across multiple buyers.
Is there real volume outside OEP?
Yes — SEP qualifying events (income change, move, loss of coverage) create year-round demand. It's smaller, but intent is high and screened before billing.
