The Problem

Why Medicare acquisition is hard.

Prescribed marketing language

CMS regulates what plan marketing can say and how third-party marketers identify themselves. Generic ad copy is a violation waiting to happen.

A compressed season

AEP (Oct 15 – Dec 7) concentrates enormous volume into seven weeks. Under-staff and you waste media; over-buy and agents idle.

A market flooded with junk

Recycled T65 lists and resold "exclusive" leads have trained buyers to distrust every source. Proof of origin is the price of entry.

Compliance

Medicare-specific compliance considerations.

  • TPMO disclosures — third-party marketing organization language present in creative and call flows where required
  • CMS marketing guidelines — creative reviewed against current guidance; we don't run "Medicare-approved" bait copy
  • Recording & retention — calls recorded and retained consistent with CMS expectations for marketing calls
  • Consent & TCPA — one-to-one consent captured and evidenced; see consent practices
