The Problem

Why auto acquisition is hard.

Volume hides waste

Millions of quote-shoppers a month means resellers can hide recycled data inside "fresh" feeds. Without dedup and source proof, you're re-buying your own declines.

Appetite mismatch

A caller is only valuable if you can write them: state, prior coverage, violations, vehicle. Untargeted volume floods agents with unwritable risks.

Fifty regulatory regimes

State-by-state insurance marketing rules and licensing make national campaigns a patchwork, not a single flow.

Seasonality

Volume rhythm and capacity.

Auto runs year-round with softer seasonality than health verticals — but it still has a pulse worth planning around:

  • Q1 surge — renewal letters and post-holiday budget resets lift shopping volume
  • Rate-increase waves — carrier filings trigger regional shopping spikes; we chase them deliberately
  • Weekly curve — Monday–Tuesday peak intent; weekend volume cheaper but converts differently
  • Capacity pacing — hourly caps matched to your agent seats, so queue time never kills a hot caller
