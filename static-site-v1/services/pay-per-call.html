FAQ

Pay-per-call questions.

What buffer should we set?
Long enough to prove a real conversation, short enough to be fair to publishers — most campaigns land between 60 and 120 seconds. We'll recommend one from your vertical's data and tune it during ramp.
Do you do warm transfers or direct inbound?
Both. Warm transfers include an agent-to-agent handoff with the consumer already qualified; direct inbound routes the consumer straight to your team from the ad.
How is call quality policed?
Publisher vetting up front, per-call scoring and fraud monitoring in flight, and a plain-language return policy when something slips through. The whole machine is documented on lead & call quality.
Can we cap volume by day or hour?
Yes — daily, hourly, and concurrency caps per campaign, aligned to your staffing. Calls outside caps don't route and don't bill.
